Effortless workflow.

Designed to feel like it was built right into the OS.

⌨️

Keyboard First

Keep your hands on the keys. At its core, CopyM8 is meticulously engineered to be fully driven without ever needing a mouse.

⚡️

Native Speed

Built with 100% native Swift. No bulky web frameworks, just buttery smooth performance with an ultra-low memory footprint.

☁️

Secure Sync

Everything syncs seamlessly via your private iCloud account. Your clipboard data never touches our servers.

🎯

Queue Mode

Paste multiple items sequentially without breaking your flow. Perfect for migrating large chunks of data quickly.

♾️

Infinite Memory

Say goodbye to lost clips. Search effortlessly through a virtually unlimited history of everything you've ever copied.

📄

Format Agnostic

Whether it's plain text, rich formatted links, images, or files, CopyM8 handles and categorizes everything automatically.

Privacy Policy

Your data is yours. CopyM8 is designed from the ground up with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit your clipboard history to any third-party servers.

All cloud synchronization features utilize Apple's secure iCloud infrastructure. Your clipboard data remains encrypted and tied directly to your Apple ID. We have absolutely no access to it.
